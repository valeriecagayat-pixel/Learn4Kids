Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about how our learning ecosystem works? Find the answers to our most common inquiries right here.

❓ Who is Learn4Kids designed for?

＋

Learn4Kids is explicitly tailored for primary school students. It aims to build foundational mastery in the 4 Macro Skills of English (Reading, Writing, Listening, and Speaking) through child-friendly gamification elements.

🎮 Is the platform free to use?

＋

Yes, completely! All interactive maps, reading libraries, game zones, and challenge setups are 100% free and open to young learners, teachers, and parent educators everywhere.

🔒 Is my child's data secure on this platform?

＋

Absolutely. We prioritize safety above all else. Our digital space features no third-party advertisements, gathers no hidden tracking information, and strictly complies with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) security regulations.
